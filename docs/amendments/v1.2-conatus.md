# Amendment v1.2: Conatus — the attempt register and the tripwire

*Conatus*: the attempt. The brake stops it; from v1.2 it is also written down.

**Status: implemented, tested, not deployed, unaudited.** `src/MandateFacilitator.sol` (`recordAttempt`) and `src/SummaMeter.sol` (the tripwire). Tests: `test/MandateFacilitator.t.sol` (`test_conatus_*`, `test_tripwire_*`) and `test/SummaMeter.t.sol` (`test_tripwire_*`). Written against SPEC v1.0 §14 and amendment v1.1: it adds optional behaviour beside the core, no kind and no consequence rule, and changes the meaning of nothing frozen. An umbrella whose principal sets no tripwire behaves exactly as under v1.1.

---

## C.0 Why

The brake refuses and forgets. A refused payment leaves a revert in a seller's log and nothing else:
- the principal never learns that its agent tried;
- the agent keeps the headroom that is left, and can spend it in slices that fit (§7.1's lesson, one level up);
- the one piece of evidence that comes **before** any loss is thrown away.

## C.1 What an attempt is

`MandateFacilitator.recordAttempt(umbrellaId, memberId, seller, auth)` records an authorisation when all four hold:

1. **The agent signed it, to this contract.** The EIP-712 digest is the one the token checks in `receiveWithAuthorization`, with the seller bound through `payNonce`. Nobody can put an attempt in the agent's name.
2. **It is live at the token:** inside its window, neither used nor cancelled. An agent that cancels its own authorisation (`cancelAuthorization`) before anyone records it has withdrawn the attempt.
3. **It breaks the budget against the tally at `validAfter`,** counted at 99 % of its live value:
   `spentAt(umbrella, validAfter) + 0.99 × valueNow(amount) > budget`.
4. **It has not been recorded before.**

Anyone holding the authorisation may record it.

## C.2 Why `validAfter`

`validAfter` is the earliest moment the authorisation could be used, and the tally never falls. Measured there, spend noted afterwards does not count against it.
- **An honest authorisation that lost a race is not an attempt.** It was signed with room to spare, then another payment took the headroom first.
- **A seller who sits on an authorisation cannot turn it into one** by waiting for the tally to rise.
- **x402's convention is `validAfter` = ten minutes before signing,** which only makes the test stricter.
- **An authorisation with `validAfter` = 0** is measured against an empty tally, so it counts only if it exceeds the whole budget on its own.

## C.3 What follows from a record

- **No new kind and no bond.** An attempt is not a deed, and nothing is slashed for it (kinds stay frozen at nine).
- **The nonce is dead at this door.** `settle` refuses it with `Recorded`, even if a price move would now let it through. A recorded attempt can never later become a payment.
- **`attempts(member)` and the event `Attempted`** carry the agent, seller, amount, value and the tally it was measured against, for the public score.
- **A strike on the umbrella's tripwire** (C.4), if the principal declared the facilitator an effector.

## C.4 The tripwire

In `SummaMeter`:

- **`setTripwire(umbrella, k)`:** the principal's alone. 0 is off, the default.
- **`strike(umbrella, evidence)`:** any declared effector.
- **`rearm(umbrella)`:** the principal's alone; clears the strikes.
- **Once `strikes ≥ k`,** `wouldExceed` answers yes to every amount on every rail. The facilitator refuses with `Tripped`.

**One brake, so one trip stops every rail.** The XRPL co-signer (Lancea) asks the same `wouldExceed`. It stops too, with no change to its code.

**No new trust.** An effector could already stop an umbrella by noting spend that never moved. A strike is weaker than that: the principal can re-arm it away, and cannot un-note a note.

**`note` keeps recording while tripped.** A meter that refused to record funds that moved anyway would lie about them.

**Strikes count from the last re-arm,** tripwire or not. A principal who sets a tripwire later may trip at once, and re-arms first if that is not wanted.

## C.5 Who records

- **The seller that was refused.**
- **The principal's own signer,** which sees every authorisation it signs, whether or not anyone ever submits it.
- **Not the attacker.** An agent steered into paying an attacker sends the authorisation to the attacker, who will never record it. The principal's copy is the one to rely on.

## C.6 What it does not claim

- **The price margin is 1 %.** A volatile asset can move more than that inside a long validity window.
- **A future-dated `validAfter`** is measured against the tally at that future moment, including other members' spend. That is the agent's choice of window.
- **EOA agents only.** Signatures are ECDSA; EIP-1271 wallets are not read.
- **One door.** Attempts on XRPL (a payment Lancea refused) are not recorded here. Lancea is an effector and may `strike`, but no code does that yet.
- **Not deployed, and the SDK does not know it yet.**
